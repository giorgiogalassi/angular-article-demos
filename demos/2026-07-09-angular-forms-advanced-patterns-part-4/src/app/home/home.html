Angular Forms — Advanced Patterns (Part 4)

Companion project for Part 4 of the Angular Forms series. Pick a section from the sidebar to compare Template-driven, Reactive, and Signal Forms side by side for the same problem: dynamic fields, async validation, and custom controls.

  • Baseline — the registration form used throughout the article, in its minimal form.
  • Dynamic Forms — a growable list of phone numbers, built three different ways.
  • Async Validation — a username-uniqueness check against a (mock) server.
  • Custom Controls — a star-rating input, once as a ControlValueAccessor, once as a FormValueControl.
